Heritage & Origins

A Legacy of Mechanical Devotion

Founded by passionate railway historians and Swiss watchmakers, Iron & Gauge Atelier began as an intimate workshop dedicated to restoring irreplaceable early 20th-century brass locomotives.

Today, we stand as Europe’s premier bespoke model railway studio. Every layout we commission is treated as a living historical diorama—combining authentic steam-era engineering with modern digital command control, silent coreless motors, and multi-channel locomotive soundscapes.

Uncompromising Quality

The Pillars of Our Craft

Four guiding disciplines ensure every model leaving our bench achieves museum-grade distinction.

draw

Prototypical Blueprints

Every curve, boiler rivet, and switch frog is cross-referenced with railway historical archives, railway company blueprints, and preserved museum prototypes.

precision_manufacturing

Watchmaker Motors

We convert legacy open-frame motors to silent five-pole skew-wound and coreless Swiss drive units, tuned for silk-smooth crawling speeds at scale.

palette

Archival Weathering

Hand-mixed artist oils, archival pigments, and airbrushed soot reproduce prototype working grime, water stain streaks, and heat-patinated firebox steel.

graphic_eq

Full DCC Digital Sound

Dual bass-reflex acoustic chambers, synchronized cylinder exhaust chuffs, realistic brake squeal, and custom stay-alive capacitors ensure flawless running.

Timeline

Three Decades of Milestones

A timeline of passion, expansion, and horological devotion.

1994

Zurich Cellar Bench

Founded as a small two-bench restoration workshop by clockmakers repairing damaged brass HO and O scale locomotives.

2005

Museum Centenary Layout

Commissioned to engineer the Gotthard Pass miniature railway diorama, celebrated for automated signalling and catenary realism.

2016

London St. Pancras Atelier

Established our flagship London workshop, creating custom layouts for private estates, architectural firms, and passionate collectors.

2026

Global Collector Atelier

Serving miniature railway collectors across 18 countries, offering fully bespoke layout construction, DCC tuning, and lifetime restoration.
